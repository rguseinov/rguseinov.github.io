I am a Political Science MA student at the University of Mannheim (Germany) and a Research Assistant at GESIS - Leibniz Institute for the Social Sciences, Computational Social Science Department. 

My research interests lie at the intersection of contentious politics, political violence, and computational social science. I primarily study causes and consequences of revolutions — mass movements to overthrow an existing regime to bring new social and political order. My work has been published in _Defence and Peace Economics_ and _Political Studies Review_.

I previously worked as a Research Assistant at the HSE University Center for Stability and Risks Analysis in Moscow, a research center specializing in the systemic analysis of socio-political transformations from both regional and global perspectives. Besides, I have taught social studies, history and political science teacher at several educational institutions, including The CTE School in Moscow, one of Russia’s leading secondary schools.
